Account Deletion - SCGS App

This page explains how members of the Shree Coimbatore Gujarati Samaj (SCGS) App can request deletion of their account and personal data.

How to Request Account Deletion

To delete your SCGS App account and associated data, send an email request from the email address registered on your account:

  • Email: cbegujaratisamaj@gmail.com
  • Subject line: Account Deletion Request
  • Please include: your full name and the phone number or email registered with the app

We will verify your identity as a registered member and process your request within 30 days.

What Gets Deleted

When your account deletion request is processed, the following information is permanently removed from our systems:

  • Name, email address, and phone/WhatsApp number
  • Date of birth and wedding anniversary (if provided)
  • Native place, Gnati, occupation, and other community profile fields
  • Family details (spouse and children information)
  • Profile photo
  • Push notification tokens associated with your device

Data That May Be Retained

We may retain limited information where required for legitimate administrative, legal, or record-keeping purposes of the Samaj (for example, historical membership records), for a limited period after your request. This information is not used for any other purpose and is not visible in the member directory.

Timeframe

Account deletion requests are typically completed within 30 days of verification.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this process, please contact us:

  • Address: Shree Coimbatore Gujarati Samaj, Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, India
