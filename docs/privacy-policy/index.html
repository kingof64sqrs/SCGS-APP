Privacy Policy for SCGS App

Last updated: September 2026

Shree Coimbatore Gujarati Samaj ("we", "us", "our") operates the SCGS App mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our App.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide when registering and using the App:

  • Name - to identify you in the member directory
  • Email address - for account login and communications
  • Phone number / WhatsApp - so members can contact each other
  • Date of birth - displayed on your profile (optional)
  • Wedding anniversary - displayed on your profile (optional)
  • Native place, Gnati, Occupation - community profile fields (optional)
  • Family details - spouse and children information (optional)
  • Profile photo - displayed in the member directory (optional)

How We Use Your Information

  • To display your profile in the Samaj member directory
  • To send push notifications about Samaj events and announcements
  • To allow other Samaj members to contact you
  • To maintain accurate Samaj membership records

Data Sharing

Your profile information is visible only to other logged-in Samaj members. We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personal information to any third parties outside the Samaj organisation.

Push Notifications

With your permission, we send push notifications about Samaj events, announcements, and updates. You can disable notifications at any time from your device settings.

Data Security

All data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections. Access to the app requires a valid member login.

Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as you are an active member of the Samaj. You may request deletion of your account and data at any time. See our Account Deletion page for instructions.

Children's Privacy

This App is intended for adult Samaj members (18+) and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page.
